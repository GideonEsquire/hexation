Hexation — How to Play

Goal: Capture the enemy Queen or hold the center for a full turn.

  • Turns: Alternate; place or move one piece. Must place Queen on turn 1.
  • Queen: moves 1 hex, can jump friendlies, captures by landing.
  • Drones: move up to their stack height, jump friendlies, merge ≤ 5.
  • Center: landing a Drone upgrades it to max (5).
  • Win: capture the Queen or survive a full enemy turn with your Queen at center.
Tip: Use the W/B buttons to toggle Drone placement.
